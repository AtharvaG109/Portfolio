## Product purpose

ProsthPlan is an iPadOS workflow and decision-support product for prosthodontists. It is not an autonomous diagnostic system, and it does not generate prescriptions.

The goal is practical: keep clinical work organized, reduce repeated administrative effort, and make review state clear without moving clinical judgment away from the doctor.

## Clinician control

- Every classification, treatment option, or generated clinical suggestion requires doctor confirmation.
- AI-generated text remains a draft until a doctor reviews and approves it.
- Original clinical files are never overwritten.
- Clinical records retain audit and version history.
- Signed documents are immutable; corrections require addenda.
- Production mode starts empty, and optional sample records are clearly marked `[DEMO]`.

- MVP remains local-first; cloud sync is not claimed.
- Product does not diagnose autonomously.
- Product does not generate prescriptions.
