## Product boundary

ProsthPlan is an iPadOS clinical workflow and decision-support product for prosthodontists. It is not an autonomous diagnostic system. It generates no prescriptions, keeps classifications and suggestions behind doctor confirmation, and treats generated clinical text as a draft until a clinician approves it.

Production mode begins empty. Optional sample records are marked `[DEMO]`.

## Workflow

The app organizes work around the patient rather than a collection of disconnected tools:

- Today, patients, review, lab, recall, templates, and settings form the global navigation.
- Opening a patient enters an eight-tab case workspace: Overview, Records, Chart, Media, Scans, Plan, Lab, and Timeline.
- A responsive safety inspector shows record gaps, unavailable files, review state, consent status, and overdue follow-up.
- Chairside mode removes administrative surfaces without changing the underlying records.

## Safety architecture

```text
clinical record
  -> deterministic service
  -> explainable draft + warnings
  -> doctor review
  -> confirmed versioned record
  -> immutable signed output or later addendum
```

- Original clinical files are never overwritten.
- Imported files become app-managed copies while the source remains untouched.
- Approved and signed records retain version and audit history.
- Signed documents cannot be edited; corrections create addenda.
- Clinical classifications list the criteria they can and cannot evaluate.
- Suggestions may be accepted, rejected, modified, or superseded, but the decision history remains.

## Product depth

The workstation includes 3D scan import for STL, PLY, OBJ, and USDZ; SceneKit viewing; true millimetre measurements; scan annotations; CAD-lite geometry; guided clinician-seeded margin detection; STL and annotation export; PencilKit media annotation; smile-design guides; shade records; implant logs; local scheduling; fee quotes; and consult-mode content filtering.

DICOM remains metadata-only. Native diagnostic rendering, measurements, and window or level tools are not claimed.

## Proof

Latest documented validation:

```text
161 unit tests
9 UI tests
0 failures
```

Tests cover deterministic clinical services, doctor-confirmation gates, versioning, audit behavior, record custody, geometry, accessibility, responsive navigation, and UI entry paths.

## Current limits

- No cloud sync in the MVP.
- No autonomous diagnosis.
- No prescription generation.
- CAD geometry is investigational and cannot drive a mill without independent verification.
- Millable crown export remains out of scope until watertightness, thickness, undercut, and validation gates exist.
